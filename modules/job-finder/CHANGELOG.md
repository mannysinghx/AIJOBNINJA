## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added
- Portal CLIs for Jobindex, Jobnet, Jobdanmark, Akademikernes Jobbank, LinkedIn (public listings) and freehire.
- Local search UI (`webui/`) that queries every portal from one search box, with saved jobs and an applications view.
- LaTeX application workflow: `/setup`, `/scrape`, `/apply`, `/interview`, `/outcome` and supporting tools.
